# Sower

A working prototype of a Bible verse feed: one verse per swipe, in no fixed order, with a short context note,
older commentators in their own words, in-order reading, and verse images to share.

This folder is the whole app: plain files that any web host can serve. Open the address on a phone and add it to
the home screen to use it full screen and without a signal.

## What the content is

- **Scripture**: the Berean Standard Bible and the King James Version, both in the public domain.
- **Context notes** under each verse: AI-written drafts, not yet reviewed by a person.
- **Commentary**: public-domain text from John Calvin, Matthew Henry, John Gill, Adam Clarke, Keil & Delitzsch and
  Jamieson, Fausset & Brown, picked out by a script and spot-checked. Plain misprints in the old editions are
  mended; nothing else is reworded.
- **Photographs**: by U.S. government agencies, each credited as the agency credited it (list below). Eight were
  confirmed as public domain on the agencies' own pages; the rest carry the credit lines given in the Fish and
  Wildlife Service's public repository and should be re-checked before any wider release.
- **Typefaces**: Vollkorn, Barlow and Barlow Semi Condensed, under the SIL Open Font License (see `fonts/`).
